Monitoring Limits & Operational Principles
WBGT values are provider model estimates (integrating solar radiation, humidity, wind and sun angle), not on-site physical black-globe thermometer readings. Air temperature alone underestimates physiological strain in Bangladesh's high humidity. Worker alerts are dispatched up to 6 hours before work windows and never between 22:00 and 06:00 local time. Expiry is not an all-clear. ACGIH thresholds protect acclimatized populations; sensitive health profiles trigger lower thresholds.
